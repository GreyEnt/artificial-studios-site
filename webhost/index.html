How we workFilmmaking is the craft. AI is the tool.

We combine hands-on filmmaking experience with a deep understanding of emerging technologies. Working closely with writers, directors, producers and crews across all departments, we bring creative imagination, technical expertise and production experience together to explore new possibilities in filmmaking.

From pre-viz through pre-production, production and post, we adapt our techniques to the needs of each project, helping filmmakers explore new workflows without having to compromise the creative process.

Generate

Create what's not there yet. We create new screen material for film and television productions, helping filmmakers realise elements of their projects that may be difficult, impractical or impossible to achieve through conventional production.

  • Locations, environments, set extensions and establishing shots
  • Crowds, background action and vehicles
  • Characters, animals and digital doubles
  • Atmospheric effects, including smoke, fire, weather and water

Augment

Change what's already there. We use targeted AI techniques to extend or alter selected elements of previously photographed material, while preserving the original plate, composition and intent of the shot.

We're developing controlled, localised workflows that bring new possibilities to existing footage and live-action productions.

Generate new material. Augment what's already there.

AcademyExperience meets new imagination.

We combine filmmaking knowledge with new tools and workflows, developing the skills to bring AI into the creative process.

Academy graduates often go on to join our production team.

Training includes:

  • AI image-making and visual development
  • Video generation and camera movement
  • Refining and developing generated material
  • Audio, editing and assembling creative content
