How a project runs

One team from brief to master. You see moving pictures inside the first week.

Brief

A call, a deck or a paragraph. We come back with a direction, a schedule and a number. Same week.

Previs

Animated previs of the whole piece: look, pace, characters, world. You react to a film, not a document. 48 hours.

Production

Shot generation, performance, VFX and edit in one pass. Daily reviews, changes made overnight. Days, not weeks.

Delivery

Grade, sound, versions and every deliverable to spec. Then the cutdowns, the stills, the next episode. On the date.

Run by a filmmaker, not a prompt.

Artificial Studios was founded by Duncan Thomsen, an Emmy-winning editor and creative director with thirty years in broadcast and advertising for some of the world's biggest brands.

Duncan is widely regarded as a world leader in AI filmmaking — among the first in the world to publish AI-generated photography commercially, and the first to bring AI into a true crime documentary for CBS and Amazon. He advises on AI legalities and copyright, lectures on AI's impact on education and employment, and ran the UK's first funded AI creativity cohorts: two separate courses, both completed with a 100% success rate.

The studio treats AI the way ILM treated the computer in 1985: as the next camera. The craft is the same. The direction, the edit, the eye for a performance and a frame. What changed is that a two-person team can now deliver what took a building.

Duncan runs the studio with Anthony Crossby, co-managing director of The Independent Post Company and a post-production veteran of over 30 years (Mock The Week, Whose Line Is It Anyway) — a creative partnership nearly two decades in the making.

Alongside client work the studio runs Planet Spectrum, an in-house channel producing short-form series every week. It is the proving ground for every tool and technique we sell.
